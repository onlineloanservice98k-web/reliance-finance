Personal loan information and general eligibility details.

About Personal Loan

A personal loan is generally an unsecured loan that may be used for eligible personal financial requirements.

Loan eligibility, available amount, repayment period and other terms may vary according to applicable conditions.

Personal Loan Features

  • Simple application information
  • Flexible repayment period may be available
  • Loan amount depends on applicable eligibility
  • Application progress can be checked online
  • Applicable terms and charges should be reviewed

Eligibility Information

Eligibility may depend on factors such as age, income, employment or business profile, repayment capacity and applicable requirements.

General Documentation

  • Identity information
  • Address information
  • Income-related information where applicable
  • Other documents according to applicable requirements

Important Information

Loan amount, eligibility, charges, repayment period and other terms can vary. Review the applicable information before making any financial decision.
